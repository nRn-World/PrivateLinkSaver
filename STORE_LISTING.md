## Detailed description — English

**PrivateLinkSaver** is a privacy-first bookmark vault for your browser. Save links behind a master password, organize them with folders and tags, and keep your data under your control.

**Why PrivateLinkSaver?**
- Password-protected access to your saved links
- Optional local encryption (Settings) and data encrypted on your device before any optional cloud sync
- Folders, tags, search, and keyboard shortcuts for a fast workflow
- Optional encrypted cloud backup & sync across devices
- Multilingual: English, Svenska, Türkçe, Español, Français

**Highlights**
- Quick save current page (`Ctrl+Shift+S`)
- Quick search (`Ctrl+Shift+F`) and Omnibox keyword `pls`
- Context menu: save link or page in one click
- Import/export and backups
- Dark & light theme

Your bookmarks stay private. We never need to see your links in plain text — optional sync encrypts data locally first.
